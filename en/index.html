The trace

What every entry carries

A defensible record is a record that reads on its own, and answers an inspector on its own.

Who a named user, never a shared account
Where from the machine, identified by its key
What the action taken
Before the value that existed
After the value written
Why the reason
When a precise UTC timestamp
Result success or failure — both are written

Append-only

You add, you never erase. A previous value cannot disappear from the record.

Failures too

A refused login, a rejected rule, an invalid entry: each one leaves an entry behind.

Chained

Every entry depends on the previous one: editing, moving or inserting something shows.

Getting started

How a project begins

  1. We look at how the work is done today. Your forms, your spreadsheets, your paper steps, your exceptions.
  2. We write the rules and the action blocks. What the system may do, what it may not do, and what requires a validation.
  3. We put the trace in. Every write enters the log with the person, the machine, the value before and the value after.
  4. We read a witness record with you, line by line, before widening the scope.
