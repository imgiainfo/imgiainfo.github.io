Getting started

How a project begins

  1. We look at how the work is done today. Your forms, your spreadsheets, your paper steps, your exceptions — and what paper does badly.
  2. We write the rules and the action blocks. What the system may do, what it may not do, and what requires a human validation.
  3. We put the trace in. Every write enters the log with the person, the machine, the value before and the value after.
  4. We read a witness record with you, line by line, before widening the scope.

One team, from the workstation to the server: what we design, we install and we measure on your hardware.
